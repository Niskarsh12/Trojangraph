## Recording tips
- Record the voice as a separate track (Audacity, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or Clipchamp), then drop the MP4 and your WAV on the same timeline.
- If a line runs long, it's fine to spill about half a second into the next scene. Each scene change is a 0.5 s crossfade.
- For optional soft background music, keep it about 20 dB under your voice.
